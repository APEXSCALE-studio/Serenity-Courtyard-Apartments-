Swimming Pool

Poolside

A quiet place to cool off

Our main pool sits alongside a separate shallow kids' pool, with shaded loungers and poolside seating throughout the day. Order a drink or a light bite from our refreshment service without leaving your chair.

Please Note

Safety information

Children must be supervised

The kids' pool is shallow but children should be accompanied by an adult at all times — no lifeguard is on duty.

No diving

Both pools are designed for swimming and relaxing, not diving. Please enter carefully.

Pool hours are enforced

For guest safety, the pool area is closed outside of posted hours.

Glassware restricted

Please use the provided poolside cups — no glass is permitted in the pool area.
