Welcome

Designed around comfort, not spectacle.

We built Serenity Courtyard around a simple idea: a stay should feel like exhaling. Every apartment opens onto natural light and warm wood tones, our courtyard garden sits at the heart of the property, and small conveniences — a well-stocked kitchenette, quiet air conditioning, considerate housekeeping — are handled so you don't have to think about them.

Whether you're here for a weekend or a working month, this is a place designed to be lived in, not just visited.
